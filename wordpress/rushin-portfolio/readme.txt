Display the Rushin Presence portfolio on a page with the [rushin_portfolio] shortcode.

Installation
1. In WordPress, go to Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin.
2. Upload rushin-portfolio-wordpress.zip and activate it.
3. Add [rushin_portfolio] to the content of a new page and publish it. Choose a full-width page template if your theme offers one.

The portfolio keeps its styles inside its own page area and includes its images in the plugin.
